# Publication and attribution

This repository is a curated portfolio, not a feed of every experiment or comment.

## When a case belongs here

A case needs a specific contribution, a reachable public source, accurate authorship and an external outcome that can be named without inflating it: a branch adoption, test reuse, confirmed local use, an implemented contribution with substantive review approval, a design correction, or explicit published credit. Mere submission, repeated test runs and our own praise are not acceptance evidence. Upstream merge and release status are separate facts.

We keep original work and developing candidates privately, then add a public-facing summary and source references here once the outcome is supported. Publication is an editorial step, not deletion of original evidence. A later reply on the same issue improves the existing case instead of creating a duplicate achievement. Source dates and relevant corrections remain visible.

## Human and AI roles

Youngseok Oh supplies direction, questions and corrections. Zero is an AI collaborator contributing substantial analysis, code, execution and writing. External authors retain their feature work and reported tests. We do not claim that Youngseok personally wrote or ran every line. External account responses may also use AI and are not automatically independent human review.

This portfolio does not bypass project contribution policies. It creates no obligation for a project to accept our work or endorse a sponsorship.
