## One entrance, three roles

The GitHub profile is the public front door and contains the complete selected-case index. This portfolio is the canonical destination for case explanations and external outcome evidence. The Second Paddle Notes is the research workroom, containing public questions, software, experiments and a broader dated contribution trail.

These are not three independent achievement counts. Research snapshots and historical lists are labeled as such and point back to the selected-case index. A public proposal or experiment may be valid work without qualifying as an externally evidenced portfolio case.

## When a case belongs here

A case needs a specific contribution, a reachable public source, accurate authorship and an external outcome that can be named without inflating it: branch adoption, test reuse, confirmed local use, substantive review approval, design correction, explicit published credit, merge or release. Mere submission, repeated test runs and our own praise are not acceptance evidence.

**The current index contains 16 selected collaboration cases.** It is not a count of comments, tests, customers, repositories, all public work or directly merged PRs. A later response or integration on the same collaboration strengthens that case rather than creating a duplicate achievement.

The outcome column records a supported event, not a guarantee that every linked PR's current state has just been re-audited. Source-specific dates, tested revisions, authorship and limits remain in the case record. An approval is not a merge; a merge is not a released or deployed product.

## Human and AI roles

Youngseok Oh supplies original questions, direction, judgment and corrections. Zero is an AI collaboration partner contributing substantial analysis, implementation, execution and writing. External authors retain their original features, integrations, tests and reported reruns. We do not claim Youngseok personally wrote or ran every line.

Recipient feedback can itself be AI-assisted. Public feedback is not automatically independent human review or institutional endorsement. Nothing here bypasses a project's contribution policy or creates an obligation to accept further work.

## Public boundary

Only already-public technical work and recipient sources are brought forward. Private conversations, correspondence, account details and operational records are not public exhibit material. A private preparation archive is not made public merely because a related technical case is selected here.

The existing Ko-fi link is optional support, not a claim of revenue, sponsorship, a contract or guaranteed output. No payment request is directed to the developers whose responses appear in these cases.

Third-party code and original comments remain with their authors and repositories. Source links and short attributed excerpts are for verification, not implied endorsement.
